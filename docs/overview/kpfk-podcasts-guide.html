PART ONE

For listeners

What anyone opening podcasts.kpfk.org can do.

No technical knowledge needed

Every program, one tap away

The home page shows what just aired and every program with its artwork, host and latest broadcast. Listeners sort A to Z, by most recent, or by category: News, Public Affairs, Arts & Culture, Health, Music, En Español.

  • Each program has its own page: description, links, and every episode, newest first, each with its own headline and summary.
  • Every episode has a link. Shared in Messages, WhatsApp or Facebook it shows the program's artwork and the episode's headline, and opens here for anyone.
  • A program without artwork gets a clean card with its name, instead of a stand-in logo.

The player stays with you

One tap plays an episode. The player sits at the bottom of the screen while listeners keep browsing, and remembers where each listener stopped, so a long show picks up where it was left, without an account.

One click to audio

It starts playing as it opens. Where a browser blocks that (Safari usually does), the only thing in the way is one large Play button.

What's on now

The program on air, its host, times and description, the song playing, and the latest episodes of the show. It moves on by itself at the top of the hour.

Today's schedule

From KPFK's own published schedule, with the program on air marked. On a phone: the next two programs.

Call, give, get the app

The studio call-in line, the office line, the station's signals, Donate, and on phones a link to the KPFK app.

Live things only

The page's own Play is the player; on a phone, a slim Play / Stop strip stays in the header while listeners scroll. A show, an episode or the full schedule opens on the main podcast site after a short note that says so, and the stream carries on in the player at the bottom there. Everything stays in one window, so nobody loses their way on a phone.

Listen along: the transcript, as it plays

The transcript scrolls with the broadcast, the line being spoken highlighted. Listeners tap any line to jump there, search within the episode, and make the text larger or smaller. For music programs, Playlist shows the station's logged songs the same way.

Valuable for people who are deaf or hard of hearing, for following a fast conversation, and for finding a quote.

Search that shows why it matched

One search covers program names, hosts, and every episode's headline and summary. Best matches come first, and every result shows the words that matched. Searching "climate" finds 8 programs and 59 episodes, grouped by program.

Re-runs listed once New

Many programs air twice a day. The site recognises a repeat by what was said, not by its title, so each broadcast is listed once, with a note such as "Also aired 4 PM". Links to the repeat still open the right episode. 77 repeats are folded in this way today.

PART TWO

For station staff

The studio: what the station sees, and what it controls.

Station managers · development · staff

Staff sign in at podcasts.kpfk.org/studio with the station's studio password. It is a private dashboard: listeners never see it, and nothing in it can change what is on the air.

podcasts.kpfk.org/studio
The dashboard · dark · example figures from a test copy

Listening and the archive

Programs, episodes and hours of audio; time listened, live listening, episode plays, page views and searches for any period; the most listened programs; problems in Pacifica's feed flagged for staff.

Import / Export

A spreadsheet or files for any span of dates, with the same names and columns for every station, so Pacifica can add stations together. One file also backs up or moves the whole site.

A printable report

For a board meeting or a funder: listening and the archive for any period, ready to print or save as a PDF.

Settings, no technical change

Staff switch transcripts and summaries on or off for listeners, and edit what listeners see of the station: name, logo, side-menu links and social accounts, with a review step and undo.

Nothing identifies a listener

Every figure is a count. No file, report or screen in the studio says who listened.

Fund drives, from the studio New

During a drive, staff switch it on in the studio and keep the amount pledged up to date. Listeners see a red fund drive banner across Listen Live and a progress capsule under the player, which updates by itself about once a minute. No technical change, no waiting.

Running a drive

  1. Give the drive a name and a goal; pick a style.
  2. Check the preview, light or dark, phone or wide, and switch it on. A box asks first.
  3. Update "Pledged so far" as the totals come in, then press Update figures.
  4. Switch it off when the drive ends. Undo puts back the last version.

Optional: a donate link of the drive's own. The studio checks the page at once and warns if it won't open inside the site's Donate window.

On kpfk.org too, in three styles

How it works

Where the programs come from, and what happens after every broadcast.

For anyone who wants the detail

It runs on its own

The site reads Pacifica's own archive and schedule. When a program airs and is archived, it appears by itself, with no extra work for station staff.

From a date to a story

Without it, an episode is just "Democracy Now! · Oct 27". With it, the same episode reads "Ralph Nader on Democracy and Affordability", with a short summary of what was said. It happens a few hours after each program airs, in English and Spanish. Where the station has written its own description, that always comes first.

  • Time markers on every line, so a tap plays from that moment.
  • Honest labels. All of this is machine-made and marked so: "names and words may be wrong".
  • Repeats recognised from what was said, so each broadcast is listed once.

The station's rules

The schedule decides

Every program on KPFK's published schedule, plus its podcasts. Programs that leave the schedule leave the site; music programs stay for 14 days.

Rights and corrections

A program the station has no on-demand rights to is held back everywhere. Failed recordings are hidden and wrong lengths corrected automatically.

Privacy first

No accounts, tracking cookies or ads. The station counts plays, listening time and page views with no identifier of any kind, and never the words people search for.

One archive: the website and the app

The KPFK Podcasts app for iPhone, iPad and Android reads this site's archive, so a fix or a new program here reaches the app too, and every Share in the app opens here.

Built for every Pacifica station

Everything station-specific (name, logo, feeds, stream, links, contacts) lives in one settings file and the studio. A sister station's archive can power its own copy of this site, with its own fund drives, without building anything new.
